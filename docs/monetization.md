# AgentOS Monetization

AgentOS monetization belongs to apps and skills.

Apps can be free or priced after SDK registration, listing metadata, permissions, verification, and Appstore review.

Skills can be free or priced after metadata, capabilities, permissions, verification, and Skill Store review.

Developer earnings are calculated only from real paid app or skill usage records. AgentOS must not invent earnings, payouts, paid usage, paid installs, or transaction history.

The current revenue split shown in the developer console is 70% developer earnings and 30% AgentOS platform share when a real monetization backend record exists.

Workflows and subagents are not monetized marketplace products. Workflows are reusable execution graphs. Subagents are user-created operators. They can use paid apps or skills, but they do not create direct marketplace earnings.

Agent Credits are separate from monetization. Credits account for platform compute usage and must not be mixed with developer earnings.

When paid usage records are unavailable, the UI must show honest empty states such as `No revenue recorded`, `No paid app transactions recorded`, or `No skill revenue recorded`.
